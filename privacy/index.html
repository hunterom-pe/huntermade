Privacy policy

This site belongs to Hunter Made, an independent web and app development studio in Phoenix, Arizona. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site collects and what happens to it.

What you send me

When you use the contact form, I receive the details you choose to enter: your name, email address, and, if you add them, your business name, existing website, project type, estimated budget and project description. I use this only to reply to you and to discuss your project. I don’t sell it, and I don’t add you to a mailing list.

Campaign information

If you arrive from a link with campaign tags (for example, from an ad), the form also sends those tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content), the page you first landed on, and the name of the referring website. This helps me understand which campaigns are useful. These values are held in your browser’s session storage only until you close the tab, and they’re sent only if you submit the form.

Hosting and form processing

The site is hosted by Netlify, which also processes form submissions. Like most web hosts, Netlify may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Analytics

To understand how many people visit and which pages and campaigns bring them here, this site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service. Umami doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t collect personal information, and doesn’t follow you across other websites. It reports only overall numbers, such as page views, referring websites, countries, device types and how many contact forms were sent.

Cookies and advertising

This site doesn’t set analytics or advertising cookies. If I add advertising measurement in the future, I’ll update this page first and ask for consent where required.

Your choices

  • You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information you’ve sent by replying to my email or using the contact form.
  • Fields marked optional on the form really are optional.

Who’s responsible

Hunter Made is a brand of Precision QA Consulting LLC, Phoenix, Arizona.
